💰 Budget BuddyA lightweight, privacy-first personal finance mobile application to effortlessly track your daily expenses and income.📖 OverviewBudget Buddy is a simple, fast, and distraction-free personal finance tracker built for everyday use. Unlike traditional finance apps that require account sign-ups, bank linking, or cloud synchronization, all of your financial records stay 100% locally on your device—giving you complete privacy, zero latency, and full ownership of your data.✨ Key Features🔒 100% Local & Private: No accounts, no sign-ups, no cloud servers, and zero tracking. Your financial data never leaves your phone.💸 Effortless Expense & Income Logging: Quickly record daily transactions with custom categories, notes, and timestamps in seconds.📊 Clear Financial Summaries: Instantly view your total balance, income vs. expenses breakdown, and spending patterns at a glance.⚡ Lightweight & Offline-First: Designed to be fast, minimal, and completely functional without an internet connection.🎨 Clean, Intuitive Interface: Distraction-free mobile UI crafted for quick daily budgeting.📱 ScreenshotsAdd your app screenshots here once available!Home DashboardAdd TransactionSpending OverviewScreenshot 1Screenshot 2Screenshot 3🚀 Getting StartedPrerequisitesMake sure you have your mobile development environment configured (e.g., Flutter / React Native / Android Studio).InstallationClone the repositorygit clone https://github.com/IzuuCode/budget-buddy.git
