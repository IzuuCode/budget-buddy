## 📖 Overview

**Budget Buddy** is a simple, fast, and distraction-free personal finance tracker built for everyday use. Unlike traditional finance apps that require account sign-ups, bank linking, or cloud synchronization, **all of your financial records stay 100% locally on your device**—giving you complete privacy, zero latency, and full ownership of your data.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔒 **100% Local & Private:** No accounts, no sign-ups, no cloud servers, and zero tracking. Your financial data never leaves your phone.
- 💸 **Effortless Expense & Income Logging:** Quickly record daily transactions with custom categories, notes, and timestamps in seconds.
- 📊 **Clear Financial Summaries:** Instantly view your total balance, income vs. expenses breakdown, and spending patterns at a glance.
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Offline-First:** Designed to be fast, minimal, and completely functional without an internet connection.
- 🎨 **Clean, Intuitive Interface:** Distraction-free mobile UI crafted for quick daily budgeting.
